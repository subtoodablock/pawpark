## (a) Monetization: how Egg, Inc. makes money without being strictly pay-to-win

### Takeaway
Egg, Inc. (Auxbrain, 2016) is free-to-play. It sells:
- a one-time Pro Permit,
- golden-egg (premium currency) packs,
- a "piggy bank" that fills up as you play and is unlocked by paying,
- since July 2023, a two-tier ULTRA subscription.

It also relies heavily on optional rewarded video ads. PocketGamer.biz called it "the new gold standard for idle game monetization" for its goodwill-first approach. Since 2023, the ULTRA perks (extra Prophecy-egg contract, ad removal and possibly an earnings multiplier) have drawn some "pay-to-win" complaints.

### Inferences
- [Analysis] Why it avoids being pay-to-win:
  1. The key paid items mainly sell convenience and time, not exclusive power. The Pro Permit buys offline time (silos) and boost slots. Golden eggs can also be earned through drones, videos, gifts and contracts.
  2. Prestige currency (soul eggs) and Prophecy Eggs can't be bought directly. They come only from play and contracts.
  3. Ad rewards scale with farm value, so watching ads stays worthwhile at every progression stage. That supports ad revenue without forcing purchases.
- [Analysis] The weak point is ULTRA. Exclusive extra Prophecy-egg contracts, and possibly a permanent 2x earnings, are power in the persistent layer. This is where pay-to-win criticism concentrates after 2023.
- [Analysis] For a new idle game, the transferable pattern is:
  - a one-time "permit" that removes a friction (offline cap) at a $5–10 price point
  - a piggy bank that turns playtime into a purchase prompt
  - rewarded ads scaled to progress
  - a subscription that adds convenience and cosmetics. Avoid putting multipliers in the subscription if fairness matters.

## (b) UI/UX: screen layout and flow

### Takeaway
Sourced detail on Egg, Inc.'s exact layout is thin; guides and wikis describe mechanics more than screens. What is confirmed:
- A big hatch button at the bottom of the screen releases chickens on tap or hold.
- Drones fly across the screen.
- There are 3 rotating missions.
- There are separate common-research (cash) and epic-research (golden eggs) screens.
- Habitats and vehicles are upgraded per slot. The shipping depot has a green/yellow/red capacity bar.
- Contracts carry a grade badge.

Everything else in the layout description below is from my general familiarity with the game and is marked [Analysis/unverified].

### Inferences
[Analysis/unverified] Layout from general familiarity with the game, to be checked against screenshots:
- **Main farm screen:** an isometric farm (hen houses, hatchery, shipping depot, silos) fills most of the screen.
  - Top HUD: current egg icon and type, cash (Bocks) with income/sec, chicken count, golden-egg balance, and a shop/"+" entry.
  - Bottom: the large hatch button, flanked by a row of round icon buttons (Research, Boosts/"Boost" lightning, Missions/Trophies, Contracts, Settings/Menu, Silo/Away-earnings).
- **Research screen:**
  - Common: a scrolling list grouped into tiers that unlock after N purchases. Each row has an icon, name, effect text, a level counter (x/y), a progress bar, and a buy button showing cost. Completed rows show a MAX state.
  - Epic: a separate tab or button with the same row format, priced in golden eggs.
- **Egg panel:** a carousel or list of all eggs with lock/unlock state and a farm-value requirement. "Upgrade egg" is a soft reset that keeps soul eggs but resets the farm.
- **Prestige confirmation:** shows soul eggs to be gained and the new earnings bonus, with a confirm button.
- **Contracts:** a list of active and available contracts with goal bars, a countdown timer, the co-op roster and a grade badge.

[Analysis] UX patterns worth copying:
1. A single dominant verb button (hatch) teaches the core loop in seconds.
2. The capacity-coloured bar makes bottlenecks legible without numbers.
3. Three concurrent missions give continual short-term goals.
4. Tapping a building surfaces its capacity, which keeps the farm itself the menu.

## (c) Idle-game design theory applied to Egg, Inc.

### Takeaway
Anthony Pecorella's (Kongregate) "The Math of Idle Games" (3 parts, mid-2010s; flag: older but still canonical) frames idle games as exponential costs racing linear or polynomial production:
- Generators cost base × rate^owned, with rate typically about 1.07 (AdVenture Capitalist) to 1.15 (Cookie Clicker).
- Production grows linearly times multipliers, so each generator eventually hits a wall.
- Prestige converts lifetime earnings into a persistent multiplier via a fractional exponent.

Pecorella specifically names **Egg, Inc.'s prestige exponent as about 0.14 (about 1/7)**. That is a very flat curve: pushing a run longer gives sharply diminishing returns. It explains why Egg, Inc. layers additional persistent systems (Prophecy Eggs, Epic Research, artifacts, contracts) on top of soul eggs.

### Cited Findings
- [Confirmed] Part I model:
  - cost_next = cost_base × rate_growth^owned
  - production_total = (production_base × owned) × multipliers
  - AdVenture Capitalist Lemonade Stand: rate 1.07, base cost 4, production 1.67/s. At 10 owned, the next costs about 7.87 while production is 16.7/s.

  — [Game Developer – Math of Idle Games Part I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i); [GameAnalytics repost](https://gameanalytics.com/?p=6634); [Kongregate original](https://www.kongregate.com/en/pages/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i)
- [Confirmed] In Cookie Clicker, building cost rises exponentially with α = 1.15. — [arXiv 1808.07540 "Cookie Clicker" paper](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1808.07540)
- [Confirmed] Part I frames the core tension: costs grow exponentially while production grows linearly or polynomially, which creates pacing walls. — [Game Developer Part I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i)
- [Confirmed] Part II explores growth "outside of the Cookie Clicker exponential model", especially derivative-based growth (generators producing generators) and other generator interactions. — [Game Developer Part II](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/the-math-of-idle-games-part-ii)
- [Confirmed] Part III covers prestige loops. A prestige is a reset that keeps some currency or persistent multipliers. Most prestige formulas are a fractional exponent (square or cube root) rather than a true log. — [Game Developer Part III](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii); [Kongregate Part III](https://www.kongregate.com/en/pages/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii); [sirpinski.com mirror](https://www.sirpinski.com/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii/)
- [Confirmed] Part III prestige formulas:

  | Game | Prestige currency formula | Consequence |
  |---|---|---|
  | Realm Grinder | √(max currency) | Resetting at the same point yields nothing new; doubling prestige needs about 4× the run |
  | AdVenture Capitalist | 150 × √(lifetime earnings / 10^15) | Lifetime-based, so you can keep resetting at the same point and still gain |
  | Cookie Clicker | ∛(lifetime earnings) | Doubling needs about 8× |
  | **Egg, Inc.** | **run earnings ^ ~0.14 (≈1/7)** | Grouped with "very flat curves" where pushing further has rapidly diminished returns |

  — [Game Developer Part III](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii)
- [Confirmed] Pecorella's GDC Europe 2016 talk "Quest for Progress" says Clicker Heroes gives prestige currency per 2000 generators owned. The Part III search summary separately noted Clicker Heroes as the exception with a true log-type effect. — [GDC Vault PDF](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdceurope2016/presentations/Pecorella_Anthony_Quest%20for%20Progress.pdf); [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/quest-for-progress-gdc-europe-2016/65405507)
- [Community] Egg, Inc.'s layers:
  - Prestige resets chickens, common research and egg type for soul eggs.
  - Prophecy Eggs compound the soul-egg bonus (+5% each by default, raised by the Prophecy Bonus epic research, +1% compounding per level).
  - Prophecy Eggs are scarce and come from contracts, the daily gift calendar and trophies.
  - "Earnings Bonus" combines soul eggs, prophecy eggs, Soul Food and Prophecy Bonus.
  - With many soul eggs, each prestige yields relatively fewer, so players focus on Prophecy Eggs and prestige during 2x/3x events.

  — [Egg Inc Wiki – Prestige](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige?oldid=9210); [Egg of Prophecy](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/Egg_of_Prophecy?oldid=7373); [Prestige file page](https://egg-inc.fandom.com/wiki/File:Prestige.png?oldid=8987)
- [Community] Community prestige-timing calculators exist that weigh lost progress and downtime against the future multiplier. — [west-games prestige timing calculator](https://west-games.com/idle-game-prestige-timing-calculator/); [onirealms prestige guide](http://onirealms.com/guides/prestige-rebirth-ascension-explained/)

### Inferences
- [Analysis] How Egg, Inc.'s structure maps onto Pecorella's model, in nested loops:
  1. **Within an egg (micro loop):** habs, vehicles and common research each have exponential cost curves. Hab capacity and shipping are hard caps. This is a multi-resource bottleneck (chickens vs shipping) instead of one currency, and the coloured depot bar visualises it.
  2. **Egg upgrade (soft reset):** moving to a higher-value egg resets the farm (chickens, habs, research). It instantly multiplies egg value, and soul eggs persist. Each egg is a "chapter" with a farm-value gate (for example, Fusion $45q, Immortality about 80 sextillion), so new content is paced by orders of magnitude.
  3. **Prestige (hard reset):** soul eggs ∝ earnings^~0.14. The very flat exponent means long runs are inefficient, which nudges frequent, shorter prestiges and event-timed resets.
  4. **Meta layers that are not prestige-based:**
     - Prophecy Eggs: a compounding multiplier gated by contracts and calendar, so supply is limited and skill/time-gated rather than grindable.
     - Epic Research: a golden-egg sink and monetization hook.
     - Artifacts/spaceships: a crafting and RNG layer.
     - Contracts: social, timed.

     These keep long-term growth going after the flat soul-egg curve stalls. It is a textbook response to Pecorella's point that flat prestige curves need extra layers to keep progress satisfying.
- [Analysis] Compounding: Earnings Bonus is multiplicative (soul eggs × (1+PE bonus)^PE count). Prophecy Eggs therefore produce exponential gains on top of polynomial (sqrt-like) soul-egg gains. This "numbers go up" engine needs the suffix notation (q/Q/s/S/o/N/d...) to stay readable.
- [Analysis] Monetization ties into the math:
  - Silos and the Pro Permit extend idle (offline) time, buying the scarce resource in a time-gated game.
  - Boosts multiply production temporarily, compressing the exponential wall.
  - Piggy and golden eggs feed Epic Research (permanent multipliers).

  Each paid lever pushes a point on the curve rather than adding a new ceiling. That is why the design reads as mostly fair.
- [Analysis] Design guidance derived from this:
  - Use cost growth of about 1.07–1.15 per purchase.
  - Use milestone multipliers (for example, x2 at 25/50/100 owned, an AdVenture Capitalist pattern; not sourced here) to break walls.
  - Use a fractional-exponent prestige (0.5 for generous, about 0.14 for Egg-Inc-style flat).
  - Add at least one meta multiplier gated by time or social play.
